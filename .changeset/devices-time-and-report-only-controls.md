A time's or a datetime's `hour`, `minute`, `second`, `year`, `month`, `day` and `hms` data points are controls of the entity, named `…InHours`, `…InMinutes`, `…InSeconds`, `…Year`, `…Month`, `…Day` and, for `hms`, by the entity alone, where they were attributes named `…Hour`, `…Second` and `…Hms`, so a switch of the entity's name beside the `hms` text is `…Enabled`; and a data point its entity reports and never writes is read-only: a cover's `open`, a lawn mower's `activity`, a vacuum's `error` and its `status` beside a `command`, and a number's `minimum` and `maximum`.
